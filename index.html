OUR METHODOLOGY

6-Step Design Journey

01

Consultation

In-depth discussion regarding lifestyle, preferences, functional needs, and budget allocation.

02

Site Measurement

On-site technical evaluation, carpet area calculations, structural analysis, and plumbing check.

03

3D Design & Concept

Realistic 3D visual walkthroughs, mood boards, color themes, and space optimization plan.

04

Material Selection

Guided selection of premium laminates, veneers, hardware, marbles, lighting, and fabrics.

05

Execution

Supervised construction work, modular production, electrical fittings, and quality checks.

06

Final Handover

Deep site cleaning, white-glove touchup, furniture placement, and key handover.
